# History

AfterNight has been developed by Ezequiel Ruiz since October 2022.

This repository's history begins with the first public commit, of AfterNight 1.0.0 on its engine 1.0.0. The record of
development before that commit - its commits, issues, plans and measurements - is kept privately by the maintainer
and is not part of this repository.

What that earlier work contributed is in the tree itself: the guides under [docs/dev/guides/](docs/dev/guides/README.md)
state the reasons for each design, and the credits for code derived from other projects are in the files that carry
it, in the provenance notes under [docs/dev/](docs/dev/README.md) and in the About dialog.
